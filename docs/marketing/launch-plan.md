# Helpado Local Launch Plan

This document is the working memory for Helpado's first local marketing launch.
Update the checkboxes and weekly metrics as the campaign progresses.

## Launch strategy

- Start in one small area before expanding.
- Recruit enough helpers before paying to attract customers.
- Promote three services at first: cleaning, moving/transport, and handyman/gardening.
- Use one promise consistently: **Post your task and nearby helpers respond.**
- Measure posted and completed tasks rather than followers or likes.

## Week 1 — Foundation

### Decisions

- [ ] Confirm launch area.
  - Working assumption: Oberkrämer and the immediate 5 km radius.
  - Use Berlin instead only if the founder can personally recruit and support users there.
- [x] Initial services: cleaning, moving/transport, and handyman/gardening.
- [x] Main customer message: **Post your task and nearby helpers respond.**
- [x] Main helper message: **Use your skills to earn money helping people nearby.**

### Product readiness check

- [ ] Register a new customer account.
- [ ] Complete a helper profile with photo, services, location, rate, languages, and availability.
- [ ] Post a test task in each launch category.
- [ ] Apply to a task from a second account.

### First content preparation

- [ ] Save three screenshots: home screen, task form, and helper profile.
- [ ] Record one 15–25 second screen video showing how to post a task.
- [ ] Photograph or record one real local helper with permission.
- [ ] Draft the first three posts:
  1. What Helpado does.
  2. How to post a task.
  3. Become an early local helper.

### Week 1 targets

- One launch area confirmed.
- Instagram and WhatsApp Business ready.
- All critical customer/helper flows tested.
- Five potential helpers contacted.
- Three posts drafted; publication begins in Week 3.
- Spend no more than **€50**.

## Week 2 — Recruit supply

- Recruit 15–20 helpers in the launch radius.
- Aim for at least five complete profiles per promoted service group.
- Help early providers finish their profiles personally.
- Spend €0–€100 on limited onboarding incentives.
- Do not advertise to customers until there is enough supply.

## Week 3 — Prepare content

- Produce six short vertical videos, three carousel posts, and five Story templates.
- Create one QR-code flyer and separate customer/helper recruitment messages.
- Use German first and English when appropriate for the local audience.
- Spend €0–€100.

## Week 4 — Private customer launch

- Invite the founder's personal and local network.
- Generate ten genuine tasks without paid advertising.
- Target at least seven tasks receiving an application and three completed tasks.
- Spend €100–€200 on a limited first-task incentive.

## Week 5 — First Instagram test

- Run two customer advertisements for seven days.
- Test “Need help moving in [area]?” against the general Helpado promise.
- Spend €5 per advertisement per day, €70 total.
- Optimize for completed task posts, not clicks or followers.

## Week 6 — Improve the winner

- Stop the weaker advertisement.
- Produce two new versions of the winning idea.
- Distribute 100–200 locally permitted QR flyers.
- Spend €150–€250 including printing.

## Week 7 — Referrals and proof

- Collect five real testimonials.
- Introduce a small two-sided referral reward.
- Contact five relevant local micro-creators.
- Spend €100–€400 only where the audience is genuinely local.

## Week 8 — Review and decide

Expand to one adjacent area only when:

- At least 70% of legitimate tasks receive a relevant application.
- Most tasks receive a response within 24 hours.
- At least ten tasks have been completed.
- Users return and no critical trust or safety problems remain.

## Weekly metrics

Record these every Monday:

- Complete helper profiles
- Customer registrations
- Tasks posted
- Tasks receiving applications
- Median time to first application
- Completed tasks
- Returning users
- Marketing spend
- Cost per posted task
- Cost per completed task
- Best-performing service and acquisition channel

## Budget guardrail

The eight-week test budget is €525–€1,300. Release it one week at a time. Pause
customer acquisition whenever local helper supply cannot produce timely replies.
